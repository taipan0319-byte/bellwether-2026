Ohio U.S. Senate results

Live results:Election nightUpdated Nov 4, 2026, 10:58 AM

Race to watch ✪

In a special election for Senate, Republican incumbent Jon Husted is facing Sherrod Brown, a Democrat who held the state’s other Senate seat for three terms until 2025, when he was ousted in a tight race by GOP U.S. Sen. Bernie Moreno. Husted was appointed to his Senate seat in 2025 by Republican Gov. Mike DeWine to fill a vacancy left by JD Vance when he became vice president. The winner will serve out the remaining two years of the term.

AP Decision Team updates
Nov. 04, 6:16 AM
Brown won seven counties in 2018 that he went on to lose in 2024. Of those, he has small leads in Wood County outside Toledo; Erie County bordering the lake; and Lake, Portage and Trumbull counties in the northeast. He trails in Ottawa and Ashtabula counties.
Nov. 04, 6:11 AM
Brown is leading in five of six counties that he and Democrats Kamala Harris, Tim Ryan, Joe Biden and Hillary Clinton all carried in presidential and U.S. Senate races since 2016. The sixth county, Lucas (home to Toledo), has yet to report any votes. Brown is on track to hold Montgomery and Lorain counties and is also ahead in Mahoning County, which he lost in 2024.
Nov. 04, 5:08 AM
When the AP called the race for Brown, Husted needed to win more than half of the remaining uncounted vote. But three-quarters of the ballots left to be counted were in counties where Brown was leading, including heavily Democratic Cuyahoga and Lucas, home of Cleveland and Toledo respectively.
Nov. 04, 5:05 AM
When the AP called the race for Brown, the Democrat had leads in northern Ohio and in the Cincinnati/Dayton area in the southwest, both regions that Donald Trump carried in 2024. In his unsuccessful 2024 reelection bid, Brown did not carry any regions of the state that Trump had won. Brown was also running ahead of his own 2024 performance in the Columbus area.
Nov. 04, 1:57 AM
Brown began the night with a large lead over Husted. Ohio counties tend to release almost all of their early and mail results in the first vote updates, usually before any Election Day results are released. This often means an early boost for the Democrat. In 2024, Brown led Bernie Moreno for most of the 8 p.m. ET hour, but Moreno took the lead as more Election Day votes were counted.
Nov. 04, 12:25 AM
Polls close shortly in Ohio. In the 2024 general election, the first results were available at 7:36 p.m. ET, with about half the count completed by 9:01 p.m. ET and 90% completed by 11:03 p.m. ET.

Areas to watch
The industrial communities along Lake Erie’s shoreline were Democratic strongholds until Donald Trump’s populism turned them bright red. Former Sen. Sherrod Brown is focused on winning them back, emphasizing his unwavering support for labor unions and his opposition to data centers. If Brown can flip Lake County, he just might bring a Democratic Senate majority along with it.

AP's guide to this race

When to expect results
The first results of the 2024 election were reported starting at 7:36 p.m. ET, six minutes after polls closed. The state reached 50% of votes reporting at 9:01 p.m. ET, and 90% of votes reporting at 11:03 p.m. ET.
How votes are cast
About 35% of all ballots cast in Ohio’s 2022 election, when the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate was held, were cast early or by absentee ballot.
Turnout last midterm
4,131,603 votes were cast in the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate in Ohio.

About AP’s election results and race calls

The Associated Press first tabulated vote counts in U.S. elections in 1848 and today gathers election results from local election officials in thousands of races nationwide. Results are reported as soon as they are available. AP’s Decision Team declares winners when certain the trailing candidates no longer have a path to victory. Vote margin shift at the reporting unit level reflects two-party vote share.
