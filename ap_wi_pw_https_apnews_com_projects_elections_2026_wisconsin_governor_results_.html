Wisconsin Governor results

Live results:Election nightUpdated Nov 4, 2026, 9:13 AM

Race to watch ✪

Democrat David Crowley and Republican Tom Tiffany are running to replace Democratic Gov. Tony Evers, who chose not to run for reelection. Crowley is Milwaukee County Executive and previously served in the state Legislature. If elected, he would be Wisconsin’s first Black governor. Tiffany has represented the 7th District in Congress since 2020.

Areas to watch
The race for Wisconsin’s 3rd District is a top-tier battleground, pitting Republican Rep. Derrick Van Orden in a rematch against Democrat Rebecca Cooke. Cooke will need a strong showing in the urban centers of Eau Claire and La Crosse to counteract the GOP lean of the seat’s more rural regions.

AP's guide to this race

When to expect results
The first Wisconsin results of the 2024 election were reported starting at 9:11 p.m. ET, 11 minutes after polls closed. About 50% of the votes had been reported by 10:31 p.m. ET, and 90% by 2:22 a.m. ET on Nov. 6, 2024.
How votes are cast
About 29% of all ballots cast in Wisconsin’s 2022 election, when the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate was held, were cast early or by absentee ballot.
Turnout last midterm
2,647,652 votes were cast in the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate in Wisconsin.

About AP’s election results and race calls

The Associated Press first tabulated vote counts in U.S. elections in 1848 and today gathers election results from local election officials in thousands of races nationwide. Results are reported as soon as they are available. AP’s Decision Team declares winners when certain the trailing candidates no longer have a path to victory. Vote margin shift at the reporting unit level reflects two-party vote share.
