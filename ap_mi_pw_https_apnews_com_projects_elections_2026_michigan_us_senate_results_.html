Michigan U.S. Senate results

Live results:Election nightUpdated Nov 4, 2026, 1:44 PM

Race to watch ✪

Democrat Abdul El-Sayed and Republican Mike Rogers hope to succeed outgoing Democratic Sen. Gary Peters, who is retiring after two terms. El-Sayed, a former Detroit public health official and progressive, has tried to portray Rogers, a former congressman, as a rubber stamp for Trump. Rogers meanwhile has said El-Sayed is too extreme for the state. Rogers ran for the state’s other Senate seat in 2024 but narrowly lost to Democrat Elissa Slotkin.

Nov. 04, 7:52 AM
At the time the AP declared El-Sayed the winner, Rogers needed to win about two-thirds of the uncounted vote, but a large share of those votes would have had to come from counties where El-Sayed held sizable leads, including Wayne, where the Democrat was ahead by more than 40 points.
Nov. 04, 7:39 AM
When the AP called the race for El-Sayed, the Democrat had sizable leads in the Democratic strongholds of Wayne County and southeastern Michigan, larger than Kamala Harris’ leads there in 2024. Rogers led in southwest Michigan, central Michigan and the Thumb area, Up North and the Upper Peninsula, though he trailed Donald Trump’s performance in those areas in 2024.

When polls close
Polls close at different times in different parts of the state. Polls close at 8 p.m. local time. The state spans Central and Eastern time. In Eastern time, polls close at 8 p.m. ET and 9 p.m. ET.
Areas to watch
In the Senate race, both parties are playing on their opponent’s turf. Democratic nominee Abdul El-Sayed hopes his populist pitch wins over working-class voters in places like Muskegon and Saginaw counties, both of which narrowly backed Donald Trump in 2024. Republican nominee Mike Rogers is courting Jewish voters uncomfortable with El-Sayed’s position on Israel, many of whom live in suburban Oakland County.

AP's guide to this race

When to expect results
In the 2024 general election, the AP first reported results at 8:06 p.m. ET, or six minutes after polls closed in most of the state. The vote count reached the halfway mark at 12:12 a.m. ET. The last vote update of the night was at 4:40 a.m. ET with about 99% of total votes counted.
How votes are cast
About 42% of all ballots cast in Michigan’s 2022 election, when the 2022 race for governor was held, were cast early or by absentee ballot.
Turnout last midterm
4,461,925 votes were cast in the 2022 race for governor in Michigan.

About AP’s election results and race calls

The Associated Press first tabulated vote counts in U.S. elections in 1848 and today gathers election results from local election officials in thousands of races nationwide. Results are reported as soon as they are available. AP’s Decision Team declares winners when certain the trailing candidates no longer have a path to victory. Vote margin shift at the reporting unit level reflects two-party vote share.
