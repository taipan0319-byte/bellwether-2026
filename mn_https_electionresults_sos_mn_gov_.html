* Results for state and federal offices are unofficial until the Minnesota State Canvassing Board meets on Thursday, November 19, 2026 to certify results. County results are unofficial until county canvassing boards meet following the election. Municipal and school district results are unofficial on this site. Recounts can be requested following canvassing. If recounts are completed in federal, state, or county races, the official results will be amended to reflect the recounted results.
