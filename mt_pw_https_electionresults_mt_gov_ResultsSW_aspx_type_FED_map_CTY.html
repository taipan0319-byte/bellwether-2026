Official Turnout Results

  • Statewide voter turnout from 1920 through the present for federal primary and federal general elections.
  • County-by-county turnout is also available.
  • Voter Registration by County daily totals are available.
  • Absentee Counts by County daily totals are available for federal elections.

Official Election Results

Election results from 1992 through the current year are available electronically. The state file will show election results for state candidates by county. The legislative file will show election results for legislative candidates by county.

Archived election results beginning with 1912 are also available.

Results broken down by precinct are available in the county files.

Election results are available for all Federal, Statewide and State district offices; and for statewide ballot issues.
