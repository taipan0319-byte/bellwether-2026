AP Decision Team updates
Nov. 04, 5:55 AM
When the AP called the race for Acton, the Democrat had leads in northern Ohio and in the Cincinnati/Dayton area in the southwest, both regions that Donald Trump carried in 2024. She also outperformed Kamala Harris throughout the state. She made significant inroads in the conservative Ohio Valley area, which she lost but was running ahead of Harris by double digits.
Nov. 04, 12:25 AM
Polls close shortly in Ohio. In the 2024 general election, the first results were available at 7:36 p.m. ET, with about half the count completed by 9:01 p.m. ET and 90% completed by 11:03 p.m. ET.

Areas to watch
The industrial communities along Lake Erie’s shoreline were Democratic strongholds until Donald Trump’s populism turned them bright red. Former Sen. Sherrod Brown is focused on winning them back, emphasizing his unwavering support for labor unions and his opposition to data centers. If Brown can flip Lake County, he just might bring a Democratic Senate majority along with it.

AP's guide to this race

When to expect results
The first results of the 2024 election were reported starting at 7:36 p.m. ET, six minutes after polls closed. The state reached 50% of votes reporting at 9:01 p.m. ET, and 90% of votes reporting at 11:03 p.m. ET.
How votes are cast
About 35% of all ballots cast in Ohio’s 2022 election, when the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate was held, were cast early or by absentee ballot.
Turnout last midterm
4,131,603 votes were cast in the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate in Ohio.

About AP’s election results and race calls

The Associated Press first tabulated vote counts in U.S. elections in 1848 and today gathers election results from local election officials in thousands of races nationwide. Results are reported as soon as they are available. AP’s Decision Team declares winners when certain the trailing candidates no longer have a path to victory. Vote margin shift at the reporting unit level reflects two-party vote share.
