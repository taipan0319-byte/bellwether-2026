Alaska U.S. Senate results

Live results:Election nightUpdated Nov 4, 2026, 1:54 PM

Race to watch ✪

Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan faces a strong challenge from former Democratic Rep. Mary Peltola in his bid for a third term. Among the two other candidates to advance out of the Aug. 18 primary is a former teacher named Daniel J. Sullivan. The New York Times reported that Peltola’s campaign was involved in efforts to recruit the second Sullivan, allegedly to siphon votes away from the incumbent Dan Sullivan in this marquee Senate race that could decide control of the chamber.

AP's guide to this race

When to expect results
In the 2024 general election, the AP first reported results in Alaska at 1:07 a.m. ET, or seven minutes after the final polls closed. The vote count reached the halfway mark at about 2:07 a.m. ET. The last vote update of the night was at 7:15 a.m. ET Wednesday morning, with about 75% of total votes counted.
How votes are cast
About 35% of votes in the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate in Alaska were cast early or by absentee ballot.
Turnout last midterm
261,705 votes were cast in the 2022 race for the U.S. Senate in Alaska.

About AP’s election results and race calls

The Associated Press first tabulated vote counts in U.S. elections in 1848 and today gathers election results from local election officials in thousands of races nationwide. Results are reported as soon as they are available. AP’s Decision Team declares winners when certain the trailing candidates no longer have a path to victory. Vote margin shift at the reporting unit level reflects two-party vote share.
